Before choosing a competitor monitoring tool

Does it support your pages?

Verify the target publicly accessible HTTPS page contains static Product JSON-LD with a supported offer, or product-specific Open Graph price tags. Prices generated only by JavaScript or after store login are not supported.

Can it monitor real stock?

It reads advertised availability labels from page metadata. These are not guaranteed to match actual inventory, checkout stock or fulfillment conditions.

Do I need alerts?

The Actor exposes comparison fields and threshold signals in its results. Connect an external supported integration yourself if notifications are needed; automated messages are not part of the Actor.
