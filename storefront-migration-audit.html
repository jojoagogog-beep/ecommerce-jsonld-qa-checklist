Six checks to run before and after an ecommerce storefront migration.

A new storefront can look identical while its machine-readable product details change. Use this workflow to separate catalog metadata quality, price extraction, international SEO, accessibility and competitor price monitoring rather than trying to use one tool for all six jobs.

Start with free, browser-only checks where possible. The optional Apify Actors linked below handle specific public-data tasks and may charge when you run them. There is no customer account access, actual stock verification, automated SEO ranking improvement, or guaranteed sales uplift.

Choose the check you need

01 · PRODUCT STRUCTURE

Check Product and Offer JSON-LD before launch

Review representative product templates for declared Product identity, name, SKU, price, currency, published availability and image references. Check the same URLs after theme or platform migration; flag changed or missing fields for investigation.

A declared Offer is not proof that the price matches checkout or stock exists in a warehouse.

02 · DATA EXPORT

Export published product prices, currency and SKU

Use the price extractor when the actual job is to capture a structured CSV or JSON snapshot. Reconcile missing identifiers and blank offers in a spreadsheet rather than treating an empty value as an out-of-stock signal.

This is a point-in-time data extraction, not recurring historical price monitoring.

03 · INTERNATIONAL SEO

Review hreflang alternate URLs

Inspect each language or regional URL's static HTML alternates, checking for duplicate language labels, malformed locale formats, missing URLs and unintentional differences between pre- and postlaunch templates.

A local markup check does not validate reciprocal hreflang across the entire site or confirm search indexing.

Try the free browser-only hreflang checker

Open Hreflang Tag Checker ↗
04 · CONTENT STRUCTURE

Check the H1 heading on changed page templates

Review product, collection, landing and editorial templates for missing or repeated main headings. Export findings, then validate the most consequential templates manually before sign-off.

One H1 does not by itself make a page rank higher; the tool does not inspect rendered client-side frameworks.

Open H1 Heading Checker ↗
05 · ACCESSIBILITY REVIEW

Find product images with missing alt attributes

Inspect static HTML for images without alt attributes or images with intentionally or unintentionally empty descriptions. Prioritize critical product images for human review rather than replacing every empty alt automatically.

Decorative images may appropriately have an empty alt attribute. A field check is not a complete WCAG audit.

Open Image Alt Text Checker ↗
06 · PRICING OPERATIONS

Start a repeatable competitor-price monitor after migration

Once the new catalog is stable, compare your reference price with a competitor's declared public price on the first run. Reuse the same monitor ID for later checks and review price or availability-label differences.

Requires supported static product markup. No checkout confirmation, hidden stock validation or automatic email alerts.
