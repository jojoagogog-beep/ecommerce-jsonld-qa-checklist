How the PAL price-monitoring workflow works

1. Test page compatibility without paying

Copy relevant static Product JSON-LD or product-type Open Graph tags. Use our free in-browser format checker. This does not fetch the target website or spend Apify credits.

2. Compare against your own reference price immediately

Prepare up to 50 public HTTPS URLs, set monitorId, and optionally pass referencePrices for same-currency comparison on the first run. Build a sample input locally.

3. Re-run the same monitor to see changes

Keep your monitorId consistent. Later runs can report published price deltas, availability-label differences, and flags in CSV/JSON. Check the output before making pricing decisions.

4. Review costs and exceptions before scaling

Billing is per priced product event plus a run-start event. A page can contain several offers. Read the current price-event breakdown and inspect the live Apify Pricing tab before executing.

The manual-work problem you can quantify

A pricing analyst who opens 20 competitor pages four times per week makes 80 page checks per week. At an illustrative two minutes of manual work per page, that is 160 minutes a week before reviewing exceptions. Your own workload may be different, and a monitoring workflow still requires setup and human validation.

Rather than claiming an unverified saving, use the editable time-savings calculator to set your URL count, checking frequency, manual minutes, setup time and human review overhead. Compare hypothetical labor time with modeled event fees. These are calculations, not proven customer outcomes.

Why a cheap Actor can still be the wrong purchase

Lower pricing only helps if the target page exposes data the Actor understands. A successful charged check may produce “unchanged” when no change occurred; paying for the same product again is expected for ongoing observation. Unsupported pages may not provide a usable price. The Actor does not automatically email alerts, verify checkout prices, or tell you how to reprice profitably.

Frequently asked buying questions

Can I track any ecommerce site?

No. PAL needs public static Product JSON-LD offers or explicit product-type Open Graph pricing. Many Shopify and other ecommerce sites expose this, but not all pages do. Verify one target page first.

Is PAL a Shopify or Amazon API integration?

No. The Actor reads public webpage metadata, not authenticated commerce APIs. It is not marketed as a universal Amazon or Shopify scraper.

Can it compare against my own price on the first run?

Yes, for one eligible offer with a matching currency, by setting an optional exact-URL referencePrices entry.

Will it notify me automatically when a price moves?

No. It outputs structured change signals. Configure an external webhook or another workflow to deliver notifications.
